About us

A Mexican partner for fertilizer supply

BDM Balmex is a Mexican company specializing in the trade and storage of fertilizers. We supply sulphur, muriate of potash (MOP) and NPK compound fertilizers to distributors, blenders and agricultural producers.

We built our covered warehouses in Veracruz for our own fertilizers, to the standard we needed. Now we share that space with other companies. They can store fertilizers or other goods with us, whether or not they buy our products.

Our head office is in Querétaro, and we work with partners in Mexico and abroad.

Services

Two services, one supply chain

We sell fertilizers and rent out covered warehouse space. Each service stands on its own: you can store your goods with us without buying from us, or combine both.

Covered storage

We built these warehouses for our own fertilizers, to the standard we wanted for our own product. Now we offer the same space to other companies. Store fertilizers or any other goods that need dry, covered storage, protected from rain and sun and ready for dispatch by truck.

  • Large warehouses for bulk volumes, plus smaller units for separate lots
  • Loading and unloading with forklifts and loaders
  • Short-term and long-term storage, with no purchase from us required
  • Gated site with a security post
